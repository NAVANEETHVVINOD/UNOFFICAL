# Play Store Policy Compliance Review

## Google Play User Data Policy Compliance

### Data Collection Transparency

| Data Type | Collected | Purpose | Disclosed |
|-----------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Email | Yes | Account creation, notifications | Yes (Privacy Policy) |
| Name | Yes | Profile display | Yes (Privacy Policy) |
| Profile Photo | Optional | Profile customization | Yes (Privacy Policy) |
| Messages | Yes | Chat functionality | Yes (Privacy Policy) |
| Usage Data | Yes | Service improvement | Yes (Privacy Policy) |

### Data NOT Collected

- ❌ Location data (no background location)
- ❌ Contacts
- ❌ Call logs
- ❌ SMS
- ❌ Calendar
- ❌ Files/Storage (except user-uploaded content)
- ❌ Camera (except for profile photos)
- ❌ Microphone
- ❌ Health data
- ❌ Financial data

### Permissions Requested

| Permission | Required | Justification |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Internet | Yes | Core app functionality |
| Notifications | Optional | Push notifications for events/messages |
| Camera | Optional | Profile photo upload only |

**No dangerous permissions required for core functionality.**

## Content Moderation

### Reporting Mechanisms

- ✅ Report button on posts
- ✅ Report button on user profiles
- ✅ Report button in chat
- ✅ Block user functionality

### Moderation Actions

- ✅ Content can be hidden by moderators
- ✅ Users can be warned/suspended
- ✅ Admin actions are logged
- ✅ Appeal process available

### Content Guidelines

- No hate speech
- No harassment
- No explicit content
- No spam
- No illegal content

## User Safety

### Account Security

- ✅ Email verification required
- ✅ Password requirements enforced
- ✅ Session management
- ✅ Logout functionality

### Privacy Controls

- ✅ Profile visibility settings
- ✅ Block users
- ✅ Delete account option
- ✅ Data export available

## Advertising & Monetization

- ❌ No ads at launch
- ❌ No in-app purchases at launch
- ❌ No subscriptions at launch

## Children's Safety (COPPA)

- App is rated 13+
- No features targeting children under 13
- Age verification via college email domain
- No collection of data from users under 13

## Intellectual Property

- ✅ All content is original or properly licensed
- ✅ No copyrighted material without permission
- ✅ User-generated content policy in Terms of Service

## Deceptive Behavior

- ❌ No misleading claims
- ❌ No fake reviews
- ❌ No impersonation
- ❌ No hidden functionality

## Technical Requirements

### App Quality

- ✅ App does not crash on launch
- ✅ App handles network errors gracefully
- ✅ App works offline (cached content)
- ✅ Back button works correctly
- ✅ No ANR (Application Not Responding) issues

### Security

- ✅ HTTPS for all network requests
- ✅ Secure token storage
- ✅ No hardcoded secrets
- ✅ Input validation
