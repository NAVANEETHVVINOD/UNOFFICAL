## Full Description (4000 characters max)

LINKER is your all-in-one campus companion, designed to help students, faculty, and staff connect, collaborate, and thrive in their college community.

**🎯 What LINKER Offers:**

📅 **Campus Events**
Discover and join events happening on your campus. From club meetings to career fairs, never miss what matters. Get notified about events you're interested in.

💬 **Real-Time Chat**
Connect with classmates, club members, and campus friends through instant messaging. Create group chats for projects, study groups, or just hanging out.

🛒 **Campus Marketplace**
Buy and sell textbooks, electronics, furniture, and more within your trusted campus community. Safe, local, and convenient.

🔍 **Explore & Discover**
Find clubs, organizations, and communities that match your interests. Explore what your campus has to offer and get involved.

👥 **Build Your Network**
Follow friends, join communities, and build meaningful connections that last beyond graduation.

**✨ Key Features:**

• Personalized feed based on your interests
• Push notifications for events and messages
• Dark mode for comfortable viewing
• Offline access to previously viewed content
• Privacy-focused design
• Works on any Android device

**🔒 Privacy First:**
Your data stays yours. We don't sell your information. See our privacy policy for details.

**🎓 Made for Campus Life:**
LINKER is built specifically for the college experience. Whether you're a freshman finding your way or a senior making the most of your final year, LINKER helps you stay connected.

Download LINKER today and transform your campus experience!
